About IFMOR

The International Foundation for Management and Organisational Research (IFMOR) is an organisation focused on advancing research, learning, innovation, capacity building and organisational impact through interdisciplinary and technology-enabled initiatives.

IFMOR aims to create an integrated ecosystem in which knowledge can move from research and learning toward practical innovation, professional development and social impact.

Our Areas

IFMOR brings together complementary activities across research, education, innovation and organisational impact.

Research & Publications

Research dissemination, scholarly publishing, interdisciplinary knowledge exchange and research support through IFMOR initiatives including IJMOR.

Education & Skills

Learning, professional development, research skills, technology-enabled training and competency-oriented certification programmes.

Innovation & Impact

Innovation programmes, institutional collaboration, organisational initiatives and impact-oriented partnerships connecting knowledge with practice.

Building a Knowledge-Driven Future

IFMOR seeks to connect people, knowledge, technology and organisations to create pathways for research, learning, innovation and impact.
